# Lich Tome Tweaks

## Changelog

### 0.5

- Tomes
  - Tome of the Construct
    - **Construct Repair** - New tome skill. Empire skill that allows construct units that died during combat to be rebuilt after combat for gold.

### 0.4

- Updated for Silver Seas DLC.
- Units:
  - Accursed Wisp and Accursed Lamenter are made draftable by the Tome of the Damned's special province improvement, Accursed Grave.
    - *This is different than how this mod has handled making units draftable so far, where the spells unlock the ability to draft the unit. But it's also more in line with how I think these units should be made draftable, as the summon spell approach was more of a workaround from many tomes not having SPI's to attach such a trait to. But I'd also like to remedy that at some point as well.*
  - Tome of Eternal Ice SPI, Permafrost Nexus, allows drafting of Hoarfrost Manta and Ancient Hoarfrost Manta.
  - Tome of Hydromancy spell Summon Lesser Tide Spirit allows drafting Lesser Tide Spirits once researched.

### 0.3

- Fixes:
  - Fixed Herald of War not being draftable.
  - Fixed Iron Golem Assistant not having Low Maintanence.

### 0.2

- Units:
  - All units in Tier 3, 4, and 5 tomes from strategic summon spells, tactical summon spells, and alternative recruitment (necromancy reanimation) are now also draftable once their respective tome skill is researched.
    - *As before, most of these use mana with some using gold or souls when applicable.*
    - *The only exception so far is the Balor, which also costs pops. I'm unsure of how "balanced" this is as while the Balor is quite good, I'm not sure if it's "kill 2 of my pops" good compared to other T5 units. But the summoning spell costs 2 pops, so I kept it the same for now.*

### 0.1

- Units:
  - All units in Tier 1 and 2 tomes from strategic summon spells, tactical summon spells, and alternative recruitment (necromancy reanimation) are made draftable once their respective tome skill is researched.
    - *Most of these units are recruited with mana and use the standard cost for their tier. A select few are available for gold or souls were it makes sense.*
  - Summon Iron Golem Assistant tactical summon spell added to Tome of Enchantment.
  - Iron Golem Assistant has the Low Maintenance and Expendable properties added to it.
    - *From a stat perspective, the Iron Golem Assistant is just outright inferior to the Copper Golem in just about every way. Rather than buff the golem to make it on par, I felt this way helped keep them more distinct while offering it a different niche.*
  - Thorned Skeletons have their hp reduced by 5.
    - *Like the two aforementioned golems, Thorned Skeletons are just outright better skeletons, having the same base stats but then also receiving the Razor Thorns property. This at least makes the base skeletons have their own advantage over the Thorned variants.*
