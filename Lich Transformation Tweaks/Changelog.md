# Lich Transformation Tweaks

## Changelog

### 0.1

- Major Transformations
  - *Any Major Transformation with a -4 damage type resistance effect had it reduced to only a -3 penalty. Tooltips are currently unchanged and will still state a -4 reduction. These changes also do not apply to unit types as a whole i.e. undead, only the transformation.*
  - **Angelic Transformation** - Blight protection penalty reduced from -4 to -3. Frost protection penalty reduced from -4 to -3.
  - **Ethereal Attunement** - Spirit protection penalty reduced from -4 to -3.
  - **Angelic Transformation** - Frost protection penalty reduced from -4 to -3. Spirit protection penalty reduced from -4 to -3.
  - **Gaia's Chosen** - Fire protection penalty reduced from -4 to -3.
  - **Gift of the Old Blood** - Spirit protection penalty reduced from -4 to -3. Fire protection penalty reduced from -4 to -3.
  - **Wightborn** - Spirit protection penalty reduced from -4 to -3. Fire protection penalty reduced from -4 to -3.
  - **Ysariel's Fallen Form** - Blight protection penalty reduced from -4 to -3. Frost protection penalty reduced from -4 to -3.
